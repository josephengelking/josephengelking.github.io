About me

Hello! My name is Joseph and I am a fourth-year mechanical engineering student at Rice University. My research interests are in prosthetics and human-assistive devices. I have conducted research at the Mechatronics and Haptic Interfaces Lab (MAHI) for the past two and a half years, where I have helped develop an assistive stroke rehabilitation glove, a haptic insole for lower limb prostheses, and, most recently, a physical surgical simulation of tibia drilling tasks for orthopedic residents.
